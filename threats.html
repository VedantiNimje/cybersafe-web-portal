Common Cyber Threats

Cyber threats are dangerous activities that can affect computers, networks, websites and personal information. Learn about the most common threats below.

🎣 Phishing

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals send fake emails, messages or websites to trick people into revealing passwords, bank details or other personal information.

Example: You receive an email saying that your account will be closed unless you click a link and enter your password.

🦠 Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, steal information or perform unauthorized activities.

Examples include viruses, worms, spyware and trojans.

🔐 Ransomware

Ransomware is malware that can lock or encrypt a person's files and demand money in exchange for restoring access.

👤 Social Engineering

Social engineering involves manipulating people into revealing confidential information or performing actions that may put their security at risk.

🔑 Password Attacks

Attackers may try to guess or steal passwords using techniques such as brute-force attacks, credential stuffing and password spraying.

🌐 Man-in-the-Middle Attacks

In a man-in-the-middle attack, an attacker attempts to intercept communication between two parties.

How Can You Protect Yourself?

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Be careful when sharing personal information.
  • Learn to recognize phishing messages.
