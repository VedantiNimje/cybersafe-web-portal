Cyber Safety Tips

Following a few simple security habits can greatly improve your safety when using the internet.

🔐 Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for your accounts. Avoid using easily guessed information such as your name or birthday.

🛡️ Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication adds another layer of protection by requiring an additional verification method when logging in.

🎣 Watch Out for Phishing

Be careful with unexpected emails, messages and links. Check the sender and website address before entering personal information.

🔄 Keep Software Updated

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated so that security fixes are installed.

📱 Secure Your Devices

Use a screen lock, keep your devices updated and avoid installing applications from untrusted sources.

☁️ Back Up Important Data

Keep backups of important files so that you can recover them if your device is damaged or affected by malware.

🌐 Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Avoid entering sensitive information on unknown or unsecured networks and use trusted connections whenever possible.

👀 Protect Personal Information

Think carefully before sharing personal information online. Be cautious about what you post publicly.

Remember

Cybersecurity is not only about technology. Good online habits and awareness are equally important.
