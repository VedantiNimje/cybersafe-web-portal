What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage and other cyber threats.

Understanding basic cybersecurity can help people protect their personal information and use digital services safely.

Common Cyber Threats

Phishing

Fake messages or websites designed to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

Malware

Malicious software designed to disrupt systems, damage data or perform unauthorized actions.

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information or performing actions that may compromise security.

Ransomware

Malware that can prevent access to data and demand payment from victims.

Cyber Safety Tips

🔐 Use Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords for your accounts. Avoid using easily guessable information.

🛡️ Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

Use an additional verification method whenever possible to provide extra account security.

🎣 Be Careful with Phishing

Check links, email addresses and messages carefully before clicking or sharing personal information.

🔄 Keep Software Updated

Install security updates for your operating system, applications and browser regularly.

Why Does Cybersecurity Matter?

  • Protects personal information
  • Helps prevent unauthorized access
  • Protects important data
  • Improves online safety
  • Creates awareness about cyber threats
